PokFulam Bank (PFB) is a bank in Hong Kong with over 1 million customers. It has a
large database containing, among other things, the contact and financial information
of its customers. A month ago, the database was accessed by an unknown hacker who
had copied all of its contents ("the Incident"). The Incident was reported to the police
but the hacker has not been found. Meanwhile, PFB's customers began to receive
advertising emails addressed to them personally from SaiWan Bank (SWB), inviting
them to switch to SWB in view of the Incident. A significant number of PFB's
customers have since switched to SWB.
Through a private investigator, PFB has found that shortly after the Incident, SWB has
purchased from MegaData Centre (MDC) the contact information of PFB's customers
("the Information"), on condition that SWB will keep the Information confidential as
it belongs to PFB. A staff member of MDC has secretly told the private investigator
that the Information is authentic, as it was obtained by an employee of MDC directly
from PFB's database.
(a) Does SWB have any legal liabilities under the Personal Data (Privacy)
Ordinance? Explain.
(b) Does MDC have any legal liabilities under the Personal Data (Privacy)
Ordinance? Explain.
